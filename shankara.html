Bengaluru · August–October 2026 · sole developer. The company title on the offer letter is systems administrator. Repositories are private. This page has no customer data, no internal hostnames, and no screenshots of live sheets.

Status: work in progress. Fleet and inventory were merged into the catalog app on 26 October 2026, and the office server runs that build. It is still being tested and changed.

The job the spreadsheets were doing

Shankara Buildpro sells building materials. Three office jobs were still workbooks.

  • The item catalog. Stewards export the item master from Tally. Everyone else needs to search and filter it. Tally stays the accounting system. This app does not post vouchers.
  • The fleet sheet. Vehicle-wise income and expenses for the month: vehicles, drivers, trips, fuel, repairs, and bills.
  • Inventory, started in October. Stock on hand against the minimum a branch is supposed to hold. The join key is the alias code. The supplier product number is not unique, so it is not the key.

What I shipped

  • A NestJS API and a React UI on PostgreSQL, with TypeORM migrations and Docker Compose for the database.
  • Sign-in and roles. Catalog upload, search, filter, and edits that stay on the sheet they came from. Full exports are streamed, because the item master is large.
  • FleetHub. Masters, trip and expense entry, Excel export, and reports you can print. People at the office tried it in October.
  • The fleet screens and the inventory screens inside the catalog app: one sign-in, one database. Merged to the default branch on 26 October 2026.

Stack: TypeScript, NestJS, React, PostgreSQL, Docker. I wrote the schema, the API, the UI, the office deploy, and the changes that came back after people used it.

What this is not

  • Not a public product. Another company would get a new build. This data stays at Shankara.
  • Not an accounting system.
  • Not finished. The merged suite is in office use while it is still being changed.
  • The public shankara-analytics repo is a one-commit visual dashboard. Staff do not use it. It is not part of this suite.
